Data Deletion

Effective date: July 4, 2026

You can ask us to delete the data associated with your Workbooks account and any accounts you have connected (including data we accessed from Meta Platforms — Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp Business), at any time. This page explains how.

How to request deletion

Email shane@shinyobjectz.com from the address on your account with the subject line **"Delete my data"**, or use the in-app control at **Settings → Account → Delete my data**. Please include your account email so we can locate your records. If you are requesting deletion of data tied to a specific connected platform (for example a Facebook or Instagram account), tell us which one.

What we delete

Upon a verified request we delete: your account and profile information; the workbooks, documents, and content you created; and the access tokens and any cached data obtained from connected platforms. Some information may be retained where a longer period is required by law (for example, financial records) or in secure backups that are overwritten on a rolling basis.

Timeframe

We confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete deletion within 30 days. We will email you when the deletion is complete.

Disconnecting a single platform

To revoke our access to a connected platform without deleting your whole account, open **Settings → Connections** and disconnect that platform. This immediately revokes our access to that platform's data going forward. You can also remove Workbooks from the platform's own app settings (for example, Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites).
