Effective date: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Workbooks ("Workbooks", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use workbooks.sh, the Workbooks Cloud application, and related services (the "Services"). Workbooks is operated by shinyobjectz, 968 NE Paula Drive, Bend, OR 97701, United States. Questions: shane@shinyobjectz.com.

Information we collect

  • **Account information** — your name, email address, and authentication identifiers when you create

an account or sign in.

  • **Content you create** — the workbooks, documents, notes, and configuration you author or upload.
  • **Connected-account data** — when you choose to connect a third-party account (for example Google,

GitHub, Cloudflare, or Meta), we access only the data covered by the permissions you grant, solely to provide the features you asked for. This may include, from Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp Business), your Pages, ad accounts, advertising performance metrics, business assets, and content you authorize us to manage on your behalf.

  • **Usage and device data** — log data, IP address, browser and device type, and interaction events,

used to operate and secure the Services.

  • **SMS and phone data** — if you opt in to the Workbooks phone channel, your phone number, your opt-in consent record, and the content of the text messages and calls you exchange with the Services, used solely to provide that channel.

SMS and phone communications

If you opt in to receive text messages or calls from the Services (for example, to text or call your Workbooks assistant), we collect and use your phone number and message content solely to operate that feature. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, or sold to, any third party — we transmit messages through our telecommunications provider only as necessary to deliver them to you. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP or contacting shane@shinyobjectz.com.

How we use information

We use information to operate, maintain, and improve the Services; to authenticate you; to perform the actions you request through connected accounts (such as creating or managing advertising campaigns and publishing or measuring content on platforms you connect); to communicate with you; to provide support; to detect and prevent fraud and abuse; and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use data obtained from a connected platform for any purpose other than providing and improving the features you enabled, consistent with that platform's terms.

How we share information

  • **Service providers** — vendors who host, process, or support the Services under contract (for

example our cloud and infrastructure providers), limited to what they need to perform their work.

  • **Platforms you connect** — to perform an action you request, we transmit the necessary data to the

connected platform (for example, sending a campaign definition to Meta's advertising API).

  • **Legal and safety** — where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our

users, the public, or Workbooks.

We do not share connected-platform data with third parties for their own marketing.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, then delete or de-identify it, except where a longer period is required by law. You may request deletion at any time — see data-deletion.

Disconnecting revokes our access to that platform's data going forward. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; contact us to exercise them.
