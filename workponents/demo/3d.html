workponents · 3d

<work-model> is the themed wrapper over Google's <model-viewer> (Apache-2.0, three.js) for glTF / GLB / STL — lazy-loaded from a CDN on first connect (never bundled). The frame, progress bar, and AR button paint from the --work-* tokens; the model keeps its authored materials. Toggle the theme — the viewer chrome re-themes. When the viewer can't load (blocked / offline), a clean themed placeholder card shows instead of a blank box.

composition-as-source-for-3D · <work-model-source> (stubbed)

The 3D analogue of <work-video-source>: a scene source compiles to a GLB via Manifold wasm. The descriptor + parent wiring + lazy Manifold loader are real; the spec→mesh kernel binding is the one deferred piece, so the parent shows a clear status rather than a wrong model.
