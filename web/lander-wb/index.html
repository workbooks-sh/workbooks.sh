Fair questions.

Do I have to code?

No. Talk, look, refine. If you do code, it's real source — open the file, it's all there.

Free how?

The app costs nothing, forever. AI usage is between you and your AI provider — your key, their bill, our zero percent.

Where's my data?

In your files, on your machine — by default. If you deploy a server for your team, it's your server: your infrastructure, your keys, your rules.

What if you shut down?

Your apps keep opening. Every workbook carries its own source, and the runtime is open source. Files don't need us.
