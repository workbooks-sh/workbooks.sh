What's a workbook?

One file that is a whole app — its data, its logic, its interface — that you can open anywhere, share like a document, and reshape by describing what you want. This page is one: the agent edits its source, the runtime serves it, and what you're reading updates itself.

Nothing here was hand-deployed. A section like this one is a small fragment the agent committed; the page picks it up on the next load. That's the whole idea — the thing you build is the thing that ships, with no step in between.
