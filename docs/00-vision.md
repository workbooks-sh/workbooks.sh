# Vision

## The fantasy

You're a creative strategist with a binder of creative components and a client who needs numbers. You compose an ad from cards, throw the ON AIR lever, and watch a compressed market week hit it live — customer dots streaming past, some stopping (thumbstop made literal), some clicking, a precious few converting. You called which variant would win before launch; now you're sweating the A/B race like a horse you bet on. The significance bell rings, the foil flash crowns a winner, and you already know why: you've started to *see* the patterns in this audience. That seeing — earned through testing, reading, and being wrong in instructive ways — is the power curve. It's also, not coincidentally, the actual job skill of a paid-media creative strategist.

## Pillars

1. **Juice is the product.** Balatro's lesson: feel primitives (eased transforms, damped-sine punches, rising-pitch ticks, ceremony reveals) are not polish on top of the game — they are the game. Build them first; the sim hangs off them.
2. **Real metrics, always legible.** Thumbstop, CTR, CVR, ROAS, frequency — real names, never invented ones, presented at three depths: meter → percentage → full table. A player who never opens the table still wins; a player who does is learning Ads Manager.
3. **The pattern is the power curve.** No meta-progression power, ever. Unlocks broaden; they never strengthen. What carries between runs is what the player now knows — which is the only honest way a training claim can work, and (per Balatro) the only way replay integrity survives.
4. **Casino energy, never casino economics.** Foils, pack rips, escalating dings, the significance-bell photo finish — all of it. And: premium price, earned currency only, printed odds, no FOMO, no notifications begging you back. localthunk's stance, adopted wholesale.
5. **Truthful abstraction.** Every Layer-1 pattern in the hidden resonance system encodes a real advertising heuristic with its real sign (urgency lifts CTR but fatigues faster; problem/solution hooks hit cold traffic; offers dominate conversion). Game balance may amplify, mute, hide, or tax a truth — **never flip one.** This law is enforced in content review, forever.
6. **Local-first, player-owned.** Everything ships in the bundle. No server, no live data, no telemetry by default. The opt-in bug report is a seed + input journal.

## Identity ruling (the tiebreaker)

**Game first.** This is a hit game that happens to train, not a training tool that tries to be fun. When pedagogy machinery and loop tightness conflict, the loop wins — because a game nobody finishes teaches nobody anything. The pedagogy is delivered in formats that never brake momentum (15-second autopsies, guess-first taps, graded pins), and the heavier instructional systems (process scoring depth, effect-size calibration, Playbook automation) are post-v1 candidates that must each re-justify themselves.

## Audience hypothesis (unvalidated — see roadmap)

Three concentric circles, inner to outer:

1. **Balatro-likes players** who want a new escalation engine to master (the compulsion buyers).
2. **Marketing-curious professionals & students** — the "I've always wondered how that works" crowd (the theme buyers).
3. **Aspiring/junior media buyers** — the people currently paying $899 for courses or $547/mo for communities that are all content and no reps (the training buyers).

The bet is that circle 1 funds the game and circle 3 gives it a moat: a $10 game competing with $899 courses by offering the one thing they can't — **repeatable practice against ground truth**. The critic's audit correctly flags that none of this is validated; audience validation is a roadmap item, not an assumption to build on silently.

## Positioning sentence

*Balatro taught a generation poker hands. Addendum teaches them ROAS — and they'll come for the foil cards, not the lesson.*

## What this game is not

- Not an idle/appointment game. Time freezes when you leave. Nothing withers.
- Not a gambling game. Chance is sampled noise the player learns to see through — variance is the *adversary*, not the reward mechanism.
- Not an Ads Manager clone. One fictional platform, fictional clients (mid-tier DTC analogs: cookware, western boots, basics apparel), real vocabulary. Trade-dress policy: echo the *concepts*, never the UI.
- Not a literal ad generator. Ads are stylized card compositions — the abstraction is the point.
