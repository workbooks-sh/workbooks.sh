# ADDENDUM — "ON AIR": The Campaign Sprint Core Loop

**Design angle:** Campaign Sprints (TD-style waves). Build → Flight → Autopsy → Shop, escalating Balatro-style across a run.
**Author track:** Core loop design, hybrid-waves angle.
**Status:** Proposal v1 (June 2026), grounded in the engine / balatro / domain / loops / cards / assets / mobileux / localfirst research briefs.

---

## 1. Pitch

ON AIR turns the creative strategist's week into a tower-defense wave: you build a small portfolio of ads from cards during a paused planning phase, throw the ON AIR lever, and watch five compressed days of market traffic crash through your audience lanes in real time — customer dots stopping, holding, clicking, converting (or not) — while you spend scarce intervention tokens on Kill / Boost / Swap calls. Between waves, a post-mortem autopsy grades the hypotheses you pinned before launch, and a Balatro-shaped shop sells you new creative components out of the same bankroll that funds your media spend. Escalating client briefs are the blinds; creative fatigue is the run-ender; the hidden audience-resonance matrix you slowly map is both the strategy game and an actual paid-media curriculum.

### Why this angle is right for Addendum

- **It resolves the real-time/reflection paradox.** Pure real-time punishes the exact skill we teach (reading data calmly). Pure turn-based kills the fantasy (launch it, watch it live). The TD rhythm gives both: unlimited calm in Build/Autopsy, bounded adrenaline in Flight.
- **The wave IS the A/B test.** Tower defense's "did my build hold?" moment maps one-to-one onto "did my creative resonate?" — and a fair-split test bench inside the wave turns A/B testing into a literal on-screen race.
- **Autopsies are diegetic homework.** Every practitioner does a weekly readout. Making it a mandatory-but-fast phase between waves is where 80% of the teaching lands, with zero tutorial smell.
- **Balatro escalation bolts straight on.** Brief targets = blinds. Boss waves = boss blinds. Shop between waves. Bankroll interest. Skip-tags. All verified loop math from the balatro brief reuses cleanly.

---

## 2. Design pillars

1. **Tension without anxiety.** Flight is short (3–4.5 min), pause is free and implied by opening any panel, missed interventions cost score — never the run. The clock squeezes; it never strangles.
2. **The autopsy is the product.** Every wave ends in a 30–90 second readable post-mortem that names *where* the funnel leaked and *why*, in real practitioner language. If a player can't explain their last wave, the autopsy failed.
3. **Knowledge renders as confidence.** What you've proven literally narrows the forecast bands on your cards. Learning is visible as sharpening numbers, not a skill tree.
4. **Casino energy, never casino economics.** Premium app, earned currency only, zero real-money randomness, no meta-progression power. Juice budget goes to reveals, ceremonies, and the significance bell.
5. **Truthy, not literal.** Real metric names, real orderings and elasticities, fixed-sign global truths — with event density compressed 10–50× so a phone session contains a statistically meaningful week.

## 3. Run structure (the Balatro skeleton)

A **run** = one agency engagement: **3 clients × 3 waves each = 9 waves**, ~50–70 minutes total, exit-safe at every wave boundary (autosave at each Brief).

| Wave | Name | Target multiple | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Test Week** | 1.0× | 2 lanes open, cheap CPMs, forgiving |
| 2 | **Scale Week** | 1.5× | Scale lane opens, diversity meter active |
| 3 | **Launch Week (BOSS)** | 2.0× | Market-wide modifier, must-pass gate |

- **Clients** are fictional mid-tier DTC analogs (Brightpan cookware → Mesquite & Co. western boots → Plainweave basics apparel...). Each client = a brief family, a card sub-pool flavor, and a fresh seeded Layer-2 resonance matrix.
- **Targets** are revenue bars at a required ROAS floor, e.g. *"Bank $900 revenue at ≥1.8 ROAS on ≤$500 spend by Friday close."* Functionally a blind.
- **Trust pips (3):** missing a Test/Scale brief burns a pip and halves the payout; burning all three, or missing any Boss launch, ends the run (FIRED). Discrete failure gates per the balatro brief — real-time tension flattens without periodic pass/fail.
- **Between clients:** pick 1 of 2 offered next clients (different verticals/lane mixes) — the run-shaping choice.
- After client 3's Launch Week: **CASE STUDY** ceremony = run won.

Boss-wave modifiers (market-wide, telegraphed in the Brief):

- **ANDROMEDA UPDATE** — echo penalty doubled: near-duplicate ads (≥3 shared cards) in the scale lane lose 50% delivery.
- **SIGNAL LOSS** — learning-phase shimmer lasts twice as long; confidence bands stay wide.
- **Q4 SURGE** — CPM +80%, CVR +30%: spend discipline vs. opportunity.
- **TREND WAVE** — one Format aspect gets +hook all week, fatigues at triple speed.

## 4. Wave anatomy — phases, timing, verbs

The atomic loop. Target median **6.5 minutes**, hard ceiling ~8 on boss waves. Clean exit at every boundary.

### Phase 0 — THE BRIEF (paused, 15–30 s)

A fax/chyron slides in on the monitor wall:

- **Target:** revenue bar + ROAS floor + spend cap + day count.
- **Lanes available** this wave: each lane = an audience with a Schwartz awareness stage and 2–3 visible traits (*"COLD — Problem-Aware. Skeptical. CPM $11. ~6k viewers/day"*). Hidden: its resonance weight vector.
- **Forecast:** the market director telegraphs 1–2 events (*"CPMs rising midweek"*, *"UGC trending"*) — TD's next-wave preview, which makes Build a real decision rather than a guess.

### Phase 1 — BUILD (paused, untimed; typically 60–150 s)

Portrait zoning per the mobileux brief: **bottom 25% card fan**, middle chassis slots, top brief/lane intel. Tap to inspect (never spends), drag to commit (card lifts 60–80 pt above finger, magnetic snap, spring back on miss).

**Card grammar (from the cards brief):** an Ad = **[Hook] + [Visual] + [Format] (+ [Offer], unlocked client 2; + up to 3 Modifiers, unlocked client 3)** assembled in fixed slots on a *chassis*. Audience is **not** a slot — it's the lane you deploy into. Every card carries 2–4 typed aspect chips (urgency 3, trust −1, social-proof 2...); the chassis displays the **summed aspect vector** and a **forecast band**: *"HOOK 22–34% × CVR 1.1–2.4% on COLD."* Bands are wide when you know nothing and narrow as Insights accumulate — your knowledge state rendered as confidence intervals.

Player verbs in Build:

1. **Compose** 2–4 ads (lane capacity caps simultaneous live ads at 3 early, 5 max ever — mobileux attention budget).
2. **Deploy** each chassis into a lane; one special lane is the **TEST BENCH** (see Phase 2).
3. **Budget** via chip-stack drag: allocate daily spend per ad from the wave bankroll; the remainder is **banked reserve** — reserve left at wave close earns 10% interest (capped) per the balatro interest model. Spend-vs-compound is the budget-discipline skill check, and reserve is also your in-flight Boost ammunition.
4. **Pin hypotheses (max 2)** from generated templates: *"[PAIN HOOK] beats [CURIOSITY HOOK] on HOOK RATE in COLD"*, *"[urgency] aspects lift CTR on RETARGETING."* Pins are graded in the Autopsy and mint Insights when confirmed. This is the explicit scientific-method loop — form the hypothesis *before* the data.
5. **Throw the ON AIR lever** — a big physical lever flick (250 ms rising haptic + transient, per the haptic vocabulary). Build locks; flight begins.

### Phase 2 — FLIGHT (real-time, 3–4.5 min; the wave)

**Time:** 5 compressed sim days (7 on boss waves), 1 sim day ≈ 40 s at 1× with **non-uniform highlight compression** — overnight hours auto-skip, peak hours and key moments dilate (teaches dayparting diegetically). Speeds: pause / 1× / 2×. **Opening any drill-in panel pauses, free, always.** Auto-pause on app background; the sim never advances off-screen (localfirst stance).

**What "watching your ad perform live" concretely looks like (portrait, one-handed):**

- **Top fifth:** wave scoreboard — revenue bar climbing toward the target, day clock, bankroll, reserve. Read-only.
- **Middle ~60%:** the **lanes** — vertical channel strips on the Late-Night Cable monitor wall, 2–3 on screen (swipe for more). Customer **dots stream downward** through each lane and hit your deployed ad card (71×95-class sprite, ON AIR lamp lit):
  - dot reaches the card → 100 ms freeze-flash = **thumbstop**, or gray pass-through = scrolled past;
  - stopped dots orbit the card briefly = **hold**;
  - some emit a spark that arcs to the click counter = **CTR**;
  - some sparks turn to coins that fly into the revenue odometer with a chip-clink + light haptic = **conversion**.
  - Returning dots wear **frequency rings**; ring-heavy dots increasingly ignore the card — fatigue made visible.
  - The Wuselfaktor funnel IS the board. No realistic ad renders, ever.
- **Per-ad readout (one-number rule):** a single hero metric above each card (40–56 pt tabular figures, player-selectable; defaults to ROAS once calibrated) + max 3 micro-sparklines (HOOK%, CTR, SPEND). During the **learning phase** (<~10 conversions, compressed analog of Meta's ~50/7d) the hero number renders as **shimmer/static with a visible confidence band** that sharpens with volume. Early numbers wobble by design — players learn to wait.
- **Bottom 25%:** the **verb dock** (≥56 dp targets, thumb zone): intervention tokens and the three live verbs.

**Intervention verbs (scarce attention is the resource):** the player holds **3 Intervention Tokens** per flight (diegetically: "buyer hours"; upgrades can add a 4th). Sim updates batch into 2–4 odometer ticks/second; everything pauses the moment you open a panel, so verbs are decisions, not reflexes.

| Verb | Cost | Effect | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **KILL** | free, always | Pull the ad off air; unspent budget refunds to reserve (spend already burned is gone). Lane reopens. | Kill losers fast. Free on purpose — the game celebrates a fast kill (clean "off air" thunk, no shame stinger). |
| **BOOST** | 1 token | Move reserve budget into a live ad. Pushes delivery; CPM drifts up with scale (diminishing returns). | Scaling winners; marginal-cost intuition. |
| **SWAP** (live iterate) | 1 token | Clone the ad, replace **exactly one** card from hand; inherits the proven cards' learned stats, but the changed gate's metrics re-enter learning shimmer. | Modular iteration — the real practitioner move (keep winning body, swap the hook). |
| **CALL IT** | free, risky | Resolve a Test Bench race before significance; banks remaining test budget. | The peeking problem — sometimes you're right, and the autopsy tells you whether you were right *or just lucky*. |
| **PAUSE / PEEK** | free, unlimited | Open any ad's full funnel drill-in (Ads-Manager-style table); world freezes. | Reading data calmly is never punished. |

**The flagship spectacle — the TEST BENCH A/B race:** one lane is a split-rig: two ads share a fair 50/50 dot stream, rendered side-by-side with a **tug-of-war significance meter** between them filling toward 95%. The race resolves on **statistical significance, not a timer**. Early leads flip — visibly, often, by design. When the meter locks: the **SIGNIFICANCE BELL** — freeze-frame, winner card foil-flashes, loser desaturates, verdict banner in real language: *"B beats A on HOOK RATE +9.2 pts (n=2,410, p<.05)."* The winner can be promoted to a main lane next Build with its learned stats intact. This is the A/B-test-as-spectacle moment the angle optimizes for: a horse race where the photo finish is a p-value.

**The market director (RimWorld-storyteller):** schedules 1–2 mid-flight events per wave on a drama curve, not raw RNG — *"Competitor just launched in COLD; CPM +40% for 36 hours"*, *"Your UGC format is trending — hook +6 pts today."* Each event triggers an auto-slow-mo window with a generous decision timer and a contextual choice (hold / shift budget / ride it). Missing the window costs the payout multiplier ("account health"), never the run.

**Two queues (balatro brief, load-bearing):** the live sim layer is non-blocking (dots, odometers, sparklines); all *reveals* — daily close-out stingers, significance bell, threshold ceremonies (ROAS crosses 1.0; frequency crosses 2.5 and a card visibly tires) — run on a blocking ceremony queue with rising-pitch counters. We never stream raw score; we manufacture moments.

### Phase 3 — CLOSE-OUT (blocking ceremony, 20–40 s)

Friday close. A "RESULTS ARE IN" envelope tears open (heavy haptic), then the Balatro scoring chain: per-ad tallies ping with rising pitch → spend totals → revenue odometer rolls → **ROAS reveal** → interest pays on banked reserve → **brief PASSED / MISSED stamp** against the target bar. Pass pays the client fee + performance bonus (account health multiplier). Miss burns a trust pip. Global pitch-drop on a miss.

### Phase 4 — AUTOPSY (paused, untimed; 30–90 s) — *the learning moment*

Three beats, ruthlessly skimmable (headline first, tap to deepen — progressive disclosure per the domain brief):

1. **Funnel post-mortem.** Each ad's full funnel laid out with real names — IMPRESSIONS → HOOK RATE → HOLD RATE → CTR → CVR → CPA → ROAS — and the leak **localized and diagnosed against lane benchmarks** in practitioner sentences: *"HOOK 31% — great. HOLD 18% — bait-and-switch: your PAIN hook promised a fix your DEMO visual never showed."* / *"Everything green, CPA rising day 4 → that's FREQUENCY (3.1), not creative. It was fatigue."* The diagnosis engine is rule-based off the truthful Layer-1 dynamics, so its advice is *correct advertising advice*.
2. **Hypothesis ledger.** Pinned predictions graded: **CONFIRMED / BUSTED / INCONCLUSIVE (n too small)**. Confirmed pins mint **Insight badges** — run-scoped journal entries that attach to the relevant aspect×lane cell, *narrow the forecast bands* on matching compositions, and pay a small client bonus ("documented learning"). Busted pins cost nothing but get recorded — being wrong is data. Inconclusive teaches sample size. CALL IT verdicts land here too: *"You called B early. B was in fact losing — you got lucky / it cost you."*
3. **Fatigue report.** Per-card-per-lane freshness bars tick down (impressions × frequency), staged like reality: **thumbstop decays first, then hold, then CTR**, onset ~frequency 2.5 on cold. Meta's literal status words: yellow **CREATIVE LIMITED**, red **CREATIVE FATIGUE** (CPA ≥2× historical). Worn card art desaturates with a VHS-tracking dissolve. Rested cards regenerate; ~3 heavy cycles scar permanently — scarred winners can **retire into "Learnings"**: a permanent small account buff, so losing a beloved card becomes progression.

### Phase 5 — SHOP / DRAFT (paused, 30–60 s)

Balatro-shaped storefront: **2 cards + 2 packs + 1 permanent upgrade**, rarity weights 70/25/5, paid from the **same bankroll that funds media** — the creative-investment-vs-media-spend tension is real and truthy. Duplicates auto-convert to **Iteration Points**; IP + a fatigued winner mints a **foil V2** with a mutated aspect vector — different and fresher, not strictly better. **Skip-shop tag:** sit out, bank the cash, take a tempo tag (extra token next wave, free re-roll, etc.).

Then the next Brief. Loop closes.

## 5. The score grammar: two multiplying axes

Defined before any card (balatro brief requirement). An ad's power is:

> **HOOK% (attention) × CVR% (action)** — on the spend you give it; **ROAS = revenue / spend** is the checkpoint metric the brief tests.

- Hooks/Visuals primarily move the **HOOK axis**; Offers (and lane match) primarily move the **CVR axis**; Formats shape *delivery* (CPM, decay speed, hold profile).
- The rare **×-tier** lives on Offers and legendary Modifiers (AOV/LTV multipliers: bundles, subscriptions) — the "polychrome" of this game, scarce and run-defining.
- Chassis always displays the chain: *"28% × 2.4% on $400 @ $12 CPM → ~$806 projected."* The multiplication is visible, so portfolio math becomes intuitive: exponential brief targets force the player toward multiplicative builds, exactly like Balatro's blinds force ×mult.

## 6. Pedagogy: where hypotheses form, test, and land

The pattern-matching skill is structurally load-bearing — every phase has a defined epistemic job:

| Phase | Epistemic act | Mechanic |
|---|---|---|
| Brief | **Frame** | Lane traits + awareness stage = the prior. Schwartz structure is real, so "problem hooks for cold, testimonials for warm, offers for retargeting" transfers out of the game. |
| Build | **Hypothesize** | Forecast bands force a prediction; Pins make it explicit and falsifiable, *before* data exists. |
| Flight | **Observe under noise** | Shimmer, wobble, confidence bands, lead flips. Test Bench = controlled experiment; main lanes = field data. |
| Close-out | **Outcome** | Pass/fail is the stakes, not the lesson. |
| Autopsy | **Confirm / deny / diagnose** | Ledger grades pins; funnel post-mortem localizes the leak with real diagnostic language; fatigue report separates "creative is wrong" from "creative is tired" — the field's most-confused distinction. |
| Shop | **Commit** | You buy cards *because of* what you now believe. Beliefs have a price. |

**Two-layer truth (cards brief):** Layer 1 — global, never randomized, fixed-sign real heuristics (hook dominance; urgency lifts CTR but fatigues faster and erodes trust; offer dominates CVR; hook rate degrades before CTR; awareness-stage affinities). This is the curriculum. Layer 2 — seeded per client: magnitudes, quirks, sparse combo bonuses. Method knowledge transfers between runs; answers don't. Optimal play converges on disciplined A/B testing — the game's dominant strategy *is* the job's best practice.

**Closure:** the **post-run matrix reveal** — win or lose, the full lanes×aspects resonance heatmap is shown with the player's tested cells stamped: *"You proved 9 cells, guessed right on 4, never touched 12."* Lessons land explicitly. The cross-run **Codex** canonizes a Layer-1 truth only after it's been confirmed in **two separate runs** — the game literally requires replication ("Confirmed 14 of 32 core patterns"). The Codex doubles as the curriculum tracker for the stealth-training positioning.

**Progressive metric disclosure:** meter-only → meter + % → full Ads-Manager-style drill-in table, player-paced via the free PEEK verb. Real names everywhere. Okabe-Ito categorical palette, redundant encodings, never red/green alone — non-negotiable for a game whose core verb is reading data.

## 7. Fatigue as the mechanical heart

- **Per-card-per-lane wear**, driven by cumulative impressions × frequency; lane populations are finite per wave, so heavy spend = re-hitting the same dots — frequency rings make repeat exposure literal.
- **Staged decay** (thumbstop → hold → CTR) with Meta's real status flags as in-game states; CPA-vs-historical drives the yellow/red thresholds.
- **Rest regenerates; ~3 cycles scar.** Scarred winners retire into Learnings buffs. Foil V2s (Iteration Points) extend a winner's life with a *mutated* vector — iteration, not duplication; the scale lane's diversity meter + echo penalty punishes lazy near-copies (the Andromeda-era meta encoded).
- **Fatigue is the run-ender:** briefs escalate ~1.0×/1.5×/2.0× per client while your binder tires. The explore/exploit dilemma isn't a mechanic bolted on — it's the difficulty curve.

## 8. Win / fail / progression

- **A session** ends cleanly at any wave boundary (autosave at Brief; flights save on focus-loss; no offline sim ever in v1). Atomic loop ≤7 min; deep players chain waves into full runs.
- **A run** ends by: FIRED (3 trust pips burned, or any boss-wave miss) or CASE STUDY (client 3's Launch Week passed). Either way: matrix reveal + run stats + Codex updates.
- **Persists across runs (breadth, never power):** card-pool unlocks via client verticals, new clients/lanes, cosmetic foils, Codex progress, daily-seed leaderboard history (seeded runs excluded from progression). No meta-progression power — protects replay integrity and the educational claim.
- **Escalates within a run:** brief multiples, lane count (2→5), slot unlocks (Hook+Visual → +Format → +Offer → +Modifiers), boss modifiers, CPM drift, fatigue accumulation.
- **Difficulty stakes (post-v1):** lower starting trust, faster fatigue, longer shimmer, hostile directors.
- **Modes:** Chill (no-timer; days advance on tap — ships v1, per loops brief). Daily Seed (shared Layer-2 matrix, compare autopsies). Agency Rush plate-spinner as a late unlockable exam mode. FM-style instant-resolve unlocks only after the Codex shows demonstrated learning — watching is the teaching channel.

## 10. Implementation notes (constraints honored)

- **Engine:** Defold per the engine decision. Entire sim (resonance, fatigue, funnel sampling, economy) in pure framework-free Lua modules; Defold is presentation only. Haptics native extension is a week-one task; the vocabulary above maps to the mobileux brief's 10-event table.
- **Determinism:** sim-owned PRNG substreams (pure-Lua PCG32 — Defold iOS is vanilla Lua 5.1, no `math.random` in sim), 10 Hz fixed-timestep tick, integer state (cents/basis points), command queue at tick boundaries = replay/debug artifact. Scripted first-session seed is just a fixed seed + fixed event schedule.
- **Two queues from day one:** non-blocking live layer + blocking ceremony queue (close-outs, significance bell, threshold ceremonies).
- **Content:** data-only Lua tables, stable string IDs, ~130–160 cards v1 (38/32/12/20/24), 10–12 lanes, 6–10 clients, ~32-aspect taxonomy across 5–6 axes — **taxonomy needs a working-strategist review pass** (it is the curriculum).
- **Art:** Late-Night Cable direction; the lanes are literally monitor channels, ON AIR lamps, chyron tickers; fatigue = VHS tracking dissolve; CRT pass user-toggleable. 71×95-class cards, taller (71×106) if the metric strip needs it.
- **Sim compression:** event density 10–50× real rates so n reaches significance inside a 4-minute flight while displayed percentages stay truthful (orderings/elasticities real, sparsity faked).

## 11. Honest risks

1. **The cutscene failure mode.** If Build decisions dominate, Flight degrades into watching a confirmation animation — the classic TD disease. Mitigation: market-director events with real choices, the Test Bench race, and fatigue dynamics that shift mid-flight; but if interventions don't matter ~once a minute, the real-time pillar collapses and we should shorten flights further.
2. **The anxiety failure mode (the opposite).** Token verbs + events + 3–5 funnels could overload a phone screen and punish reflection. Free-pause-on-peek is the safety valve, but if testers pause constantly the "live" fantasy is dead and Chill mode becomes the real game — a signal we mistuned, not a feature.
3. **Autopsy as homework.** A mandatory reading phase every ~6 minutes can feel like school. It must be skimmable in 15 s with depth on tap; if retention data shows autopsy-skipping, the teaching pillar fails silently.
4. **Noise vs. fairness.** Statistical wobble that teaches patience reads as RNG theft when it costs a trust pip. Wave-level outcomes must be dominated by decision quality (Layer-1 truths), with noise mostly inside the wave; getting this ratio wrong either kills learnability or kills trust in the game.
5. **Two clocks can desync.** Significance-based race resolution vs. fixed-length waves means some tests end INCONCLUSIVE at Friday close. That's pedagogically *correct* (underpowered tests are real) but emotionally flat; needs a fair carry-over rule (bench tests can resume next wave with accumulated n).
6. **Portfolio readability on portrait.** 4–5 simultaneous lanes may simply not fit one-handed portrait; we may be forced down to 2–3 visible live ads, weakening the "manage a portfolio" fantasy. Prototype dots-vs-dashboard density first.
7. **Run length.** 9 waves ≈ 50–70 min is long for mobile norms even with clean wave-boundary exits; flights themselves (3–4.5 min) are interruption-fragile. May need a 2-client "short engagement" run as default.
8. **Bespoke-systems scope for a solo dev.** Live sim + ceremony queue + diagnosis engine + hypothesis grader + market director is five custom systems before content. The diagnosis engine especially has no off-the-shelf analog.
9. **The educational claim raises the bar permanently.** Every balance change must preserve Layer-1 truthfulness; a fun-motivated tweak that flips a real heuristic (e.g., making urgency fatigue-free) corrupts the curriculum. Needs a standing practitioner review gate in the content pipeline.
10. **Seeded Layer-2 can read as "the game lied."** If a per-client quirk contradicts a player's hard-won prior (legitimately — quirks are the point), the autopsy must explicitly label it (*"this audience is unusual: …"*) or players will distrust the Layer-1 lessons too.

## 12. Open questions for the next pass

- Do V2 foils ever get to be strictly better (cards brief open question) — current stance: never.
- Short-run (2-client) vs full-run (3-client) as the default mode — playtest call.
