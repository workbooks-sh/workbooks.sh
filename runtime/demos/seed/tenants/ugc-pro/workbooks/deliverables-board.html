The production rule this board enforces. A text-overlay / caption / on-screen-copy change is a revision of the same deliverable (move it to Revision, keep the deal count). A reshoot — new footage, new setup, re-filmed b-roll — is a new deliverable on the deal, not a revision. The drawer's two buttons are the only sanctioned paths, so usage-rights billing and the "x of N" bundle counts never drift.
