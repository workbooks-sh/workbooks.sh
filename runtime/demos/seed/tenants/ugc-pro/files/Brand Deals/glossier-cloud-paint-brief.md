## 1. Overview

Glossier wants 3 short-form videos that make Cloud Paint feel like the easiest
*"I woke up like this"* flush — **not** a makeup tutorial. The vibe is a real
morning, not a studio. Bathroom-window light, no ring light, no overlined lips,
no "today I'm going to show you." We're buying authenticity; if it looks like an
ad, it failed.

The goal is paid-social performance: these run as Spark Ads against a cold TikTok
audience for 90 days. We're measuring **3-second hold rate** and **CTR to PDP**,
so the first two seconds carry the whole deal.

## 2. Audience

Women **18–32**, lapsed and net-new Glossier customers, scrolling TikTok in the
morning or on a commute. Skin-first, low-effort, "clean girl" adjacent but tired
of 12-step routines. They distrust polished beauty ads and respond to a real face
with a real problem.

## 3. Deliverables

- **3 videos**, 20–30s each, vertical **9:16, 1080×1920**, 30fps.
- **2 alternative hooks per video** (6 total) for A/B testing — delivered as
  separate clips, same body.
- **Captions burned in**, TikTok-native style (not corporate lower-thirds).
- Raw footage **not** required this round.
- File naming: `glossier-cloudpaint-v{1-3}-hook{a,b}.mp4`.

## 4. Creative direction — the beat

Every video follows the same five-beat spine. The angle changes per video; the
spine doesn't.

1. **Hook (0–2s)** — face on camera, *stop the scroll*. Examples:
   - "POV: your skin barrier finally healed and now you just need *one* thing."
   - "I deleted my whole makeup bag for this."
   - "the blush that made my coworker ask if I 'did something.'"
2. **Problem (2–6s)** — "Every blush I owned looked cakey by noon."
3. **Demo (6–16s)** — two-finger tap of Cloud Paint, blend with fingertips,
   show the dewy, skin-like finish. No brush. Keep hands in frame.
4. **Proof (16–24s)** — end-of-day mirror check: still there, still natural,
   never powdery.
5. **CTA (24–30s)** — soft. "Shade is Beam. Linked it." No hard sell, no
   "run don't walk," no discount code energy.

**Per-video angles**
- **V1 — the convert:** ex-cream-blush-skeptic who got won over.
- **V2 — the 5-second routine:** speed as the whole pitch.
- **V3 — the longevity test:** morning application → 6pm proof shot.

## 5. Tone & messaging frameworks (do **NOT** read verbatim)

- "barely-there but it's doing the most"
- "looks like your skin, but better"
- "the 5-second flush"
- "a wash of color, not a layer of makeup"

**Avoid:** SKU codes, "this product features…", "obsessed isn't a strong enough
word," overproduced ad voice, transitions, trending-but-irrelevant audio.

## 6. Technical specs

- **Natural light only.** No smoothing filters, no skin-blur — texture is the point.
- **Audio:** trending TikTok audio **or** clean to-camera voiceover. No
  copyrighted music. Provide the audio name/ID if trending audio is used.
- Show the product **clearly for ≥3 seconds total** (Glossier brand requirement).
- Shade shown is **Beam**. Do not feature other Glossier products in frame.
- Safe margins: keep captions clear of the bottom 240px (TikTok UI).
