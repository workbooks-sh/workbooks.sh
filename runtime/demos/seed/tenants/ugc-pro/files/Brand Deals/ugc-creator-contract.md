## 1. Scope of work

Creator will produce **[#] video(s)** and **[#] image(s)** as described in the
attached brief ("Deliverables"), each **[length]** seconds, formatted
**[9:16 / 1:1 / 4:5]** at **1080×1920 / [spec]** for **[platform(s)]**. The brief
governs creative direction; this agreement governs the business terms. Where they
conflict, this agreement controls on **fees, rights, and timeline**.

## 2. Fees & payment

- **Total fee:** **$[amount]**.
- **50% deposit** due on signing to reserve the shoot date; this deposit is
  **non-refundable** once scripts/concepts are approved.
- Remaining **50%** is **net-7** after final delivery and acceptance.
- Late payment after **14 days** accrues **1.5% per month**.
- Payment via **[ACH / Wise / Stripe invoice]** to the details on the invoice.

## 3. Deliverables & timeline

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Scripts / concepts approved | [date] |
| Shoot day | [date] |
| First cut delivered | [date] |
| Brand revision notes due | [date] |
| **Final delivery** | **[date]** |

Standard turnaround is **5–10 business days** from script approval. **Rush
(<72h)** adds **35%**. Brand delays in approval extend the final date day-for-day.

## 4. Revisions

- **2 rounds** of minor revisions are included (timing, music, framing, caption,
  on-screen text).
- A text-overlay or caption change is a **revision**. A reshoot, new hook concept,
  or new angle is a **new deliverable**, billed at the rate-card rate.
- Additional revision rounds beyond the included two are **$90/round**.

## 5. Usage rights

Rights are licensed, not sold. Each license is time-boxed and reverts to Creator
at term end.

| Right | Scope | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| **Organic** social (Brand's owned channels) | Perpetual | Included |
| **Paid** (boosted / Spark Ads / Meta / TikTok Ads) | **[90] days** from delivery | **+40%** of base |
| **Whitelisting** (ads served from Creator's handle) | Per month | **+30%** of base / mo |
| **Perpetual** paid rights | Forever | **2.5×** the 90-day rate |

Brand may not (a) resell or sublicense the content, (b) edit it in a way that
materially changes the message or pairs it with a competing product, or (c) run
it past the licensed term without renewal.

## 6. Raw footage

Raw, unedited footage and selects are **not** included unless purchased at
**+35%** of base. Delivered as-shot with no color or audio treatment.

## 7. Exclusivity

Unless stated here, this engagement is **non-exclusive**. Category exclusivity
(e.g. no competing **[category]** brand for **[30]** days) is **+25%** of fee
and must be specified: **[ ] none  [ ] 30 days  [ ] other: ______**.

## 8. Approvals & FTC disclosure

Brand approves the final cut before publishing. Creator will include all required
disclosures (**#ad** / "Paid partnership" label) per **FTC Endorsement Guides**
and platform policy. Brand may not strip or obscure required disclosures. Creator
warrants the content is original and clears no third-party music/IP without a
license provided by Brand.

## 9. Cancellation

- If Brand cancels **before** scripts are approved: deposit refunded less a
  **$150** booking fee.
- If Brand cancels **after** scripts are approved: deposit is **non-refundable**.
- If Brand cancels **after** shooting: **75%** of total fee is due.

## 10. Ownership & confidentiality

Creator retains copyright in all footage; Brand receives the license in §5. Each
party keeps the other's non-public information (briefs, rates, unreleased
products) confidential. Neither party speaks for the other publicly without
consent, except Creator may show delivered work in their **portfolio** unless
Brand opts out in writing here: **[ ] portfolio use not permitted**.

## 11. Independent contractor

Creator is an independent contractor, not an employee. Creator is responsible for
their own taxes, gear, and insurance. Governing law: **[state/country]**.
