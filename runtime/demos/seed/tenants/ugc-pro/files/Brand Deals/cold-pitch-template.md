# The 4-Line Cold Pitch (11% reply rate)

UGC Pro community swipe. Across our members' tracked sends, cold-outreach reply
rates sit at **5–15%** — this 4-line structure pulls **~11%** versus ~4% for the
"I'm a passionate content creator…" paragraph that everyone else sends. Short,
specific, and aimed at the right inbox.

> **Aim it right.** Pitch a **person who owns the creator budget** — not
> `info@`, `press@`, or `hello@`. Find the **Creator Partnerships / Influencer
> Lead / Brand Marketing** contact on LinkedIn, then guess the email pattern
> (`first@`, `first.last@`, `flast@`) and verify it. The pitch is only as good as
> the inbox it lands in.

---

## The template

**Subject:** Quick idea for [Brand]'s [product]

> Hi [First name],
>
> I make scroll-stopping UGC for beauty/wellness brands (recently **Glossier,
> Ritual, Olipop**) — short, native, built to convert in paid.
>
> I have a hook idea for **[specific product]**: *"[one-line hook]"*. I think it'd
> out-perform a studio ad for your **[TikTok / Reels]** cold audience.
>
> 90-second portfolio here → **[link]**. Want me to send a quick concept + rate?
>
> — Kelly

---

## Why each line works

| Line | What it does | Why it lands |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Credibility** | Names real brands you've worked with | Specific proof beats adjectives. "Recently Glossier" > "I'm passionate about content." |
| **2 — Specific idea** | A hook for *their* product, by name | Shows you researched them. Generic offers read as spam. |
| **3 — The bet** | "out-perform a studio ad … cold audience" | Speaks their language — performance, not vanity reach. |
| **4 — Low-friction CTA** | "Want me to send a concept + rate?" | "Want me to send" converts higher than "let's hop on a call." You earn the meeting. |

**Rules:** under **80 words**. **No rate in the first email** — you earn the
right to quote. One idea, one ask. No attachments on touch 1 (kills deliverability).

---

## Follow-up cadence

Most replies come on touch 2 or 3 — the pitch isn't dead, it's buried.

| Touch | Day | Angle | Attach? |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | The pitch above | No |
| 2 | +3 | "Bumping this — here's the hook as a 15s sample" | Yes — the spec clip |
| 3 | +7 | "Last nudge — happy to send a free spec concept if useful" | No |

After touch 3 with no reply: mark **`pitched`** in the outreach sheet and re-pitch
in **60 days** with a new product angle. Never break up dramatically ("guess
you're not interested") — it burns the contact.

---

## The volume math

> Sending 10 emails a week doesn't move the needle. Batch **40–60/week**, track
> every touch in your outreach sheet, and let the **11%** compound.

- 50 sends/week × 11% reply = **~5–6 conversations/week**.
- ~1 in 3 good conversations books → **~2 new deals/week** at full pipeline.
- A booked beauty/wellness deal averages **~$1,400** (see the earnings ledger).
- That's the whole business: *right inbox × specific idea × relentless follow-up.*
