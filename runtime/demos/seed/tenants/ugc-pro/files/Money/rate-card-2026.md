## Terms

- **Deposit:** 50% to reserve a shoot date; balance **net-7** on final delivery.
- **Late payment:** invoices unpaid after 14 days accrue **1.5%/month**.
- **Usage rights** are time-boxed. Perpetual rights are priced on request —
  typically **2.5×** the 90-day rate. Rights revert to me at term end.
- **Scope creep kills margins.** Anything past 2 revision rounds is billed at
  **$90/round**. A reshoot is a new deliverable.
- **Cancellation:** deposit is non-refundable once scripts are approved; **75%**
  of fee is due if cancelled after shooting.
- **FTC:** every paid piece carries the required #ad / paid-partnership label.
- **Booking** June–August 2026 now open. I reply to every inquiry within **48h**.
