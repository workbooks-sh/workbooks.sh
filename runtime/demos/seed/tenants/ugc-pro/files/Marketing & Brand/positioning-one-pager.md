# UGC Pro — Positioning

**Tagline:** Create content that converts.

**Who:** Professional UGC creators and the brands that hire them.

**Promise:** Hook-first, performance-graded short-form that earns the watch and
moves the metric — not vanity views.

## Pillars
1. **Hook science** — first second decides the spend. Every concept opens on a
   named problem or a face-first POV.
2. **Performance read** — every deliverable ships with a hold-rate target and a
   post-flight read against paid CTR.
3. **Operator-grade ops** — pipeline, deliverables board, rate card, and a real
   book of business behind the content.

## Voice
Direct, creator-native, zero corporate gloss. Show the work; name the metric.
