Why list price matters

The pricing pyramid

A list price is a marketing decision, not a wish. Where we set it determines how many buyers ever see the home — and the right price reaches the most.

Buyers shop in price bands. Set the number too high and you skip past the people searching just below it — the largest pools never even tour.

Pricing at market value sits the home in front of roughly 90% of qualified buyers, the level that produces showings, then offers, then competition.

Overprice "to leave room to negotiate" and the home sits, goes stale, and ultimately sells for less after a price cut. In a 1.6-month market, accurate pricing is the aggressive move.
