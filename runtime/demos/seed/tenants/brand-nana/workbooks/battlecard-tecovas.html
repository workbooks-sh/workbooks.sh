▲ How they win

Brand heat & cultural pull Demand
Owns the "modern Western" aesthetic with millennial/Gen-Z reach. ~1.1M Instagram followers, celeb & rodeo-circuit collabs, and the strongest unaided recall in DTC Western. Western-wear tailwind is real.
Frictionless DTC promise CX
Free shipping both ways and a 14-day wear-them-outside return guarantee. Removes the #1 objection to buying $300 boots online — fit risk.
Retail expansion Distribution
50+ owned stores across TX, the Sun Belt & expanding nationally. Lets shoppers try-on, which closes the fit gap that hurts pure online — and lifts AOV via apparel attach.
Broad, full-look catalog Basket
Boots → jeans, shirts, hats, belts, bags, kids. One-brand "head-to-toe Western" raises items-per-order and repeat rate beyond a boots-only buyer.

▼ Where they're vulnerable

Premium price vs. Ariat Price
Core boots land $235–$345 while Ariat covers the $130–$200 heartland with comparable durability and far wider distribution. Tecovas is squeezed: pricier than mass, cheaper-feeling than Lucchese.
Narrow fit / width options Fit
Effectively a single (medium / D-ish) width for most styles. Buyers with wide or narrow feet are stranded — Ariat ships true B/D/EE widths. Top driver of their returns.
Quality / wear complaints Quality
Recurring reviews flag thin insole comfort over long days, sole-bonding separation, and pilling on knit/wool apparel after a few washes. Erodes the "heirloom" claim they charge a premium for.
Online-first fit risk Channel
Outside its ~50 store metros, it's still buy-blind-and-return. In wide-width or work-boot use cases, that's a hard "no" your in-store / true-width offer can win.

◆ How to position

1Win on fit & widths
Tecovas makes a beautiful boot — in one width. We fit B through EE, so you're not gambling on a $300 return. Let's get you measured and into the right last today.
Use when: buyer mentions wide/narrow feet, all-day wear, or "I returned my last online boots."
2Reframe price as cost-per-wear
At $300 you're paying brand tax. Spend it on resoleable construction and the right fit and your cost-per-wear is half — boots you keep five years, not one season.
Use when: they're anchored on the Tecovas price or comparing on sticker alone.
3Sell the proof, not the promise
"Heirloom" is the pitch — but check the reviews on sole separation and insole comfort. We back ours with a resole program and a real comfort system, in writing.
Use when: buyer is quality-conscious or already half-sold on Tecovas's story.
⚠

Landmines — do not step here

✕
Don't compete on the heritage story — they own it. Their brand, content, and "modern Western" narrative are best-in-class. Attack story and you lose; redirect to fit, widths, and durability proof.
✕
Don't trash the boots outright. The leather and look are genuinely good — buyers know it. Acknowledge the quality, then move to where you're objectively better (width range, resole, comfort guarantee).
✕
Don't fight on free shipping/returns. You won't out-logistics a funded DTC. Make the offer moot — get fit right the first time so there's nothing to return.
