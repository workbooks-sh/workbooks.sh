◐ Where Allbirds wins

  • Comfort authority. "World's most comfortable" is owned territory; merino + Trino feel is the moat.
  • Price accessibility. Entry at $98 undercuts Rothy's entire line — broadest reachable audience.
  • Carbon literacy. Per-product CO₂e label on every PDP — sustainability you can measure, not just claim.
  • Category breadth. Runners, trainers, slip-ons, apparel & socks vs. a flats-anchored core.

◑ Where Rothy's wins

  • Design distinctiveness. The seamless knit silhouette is instantly recognizable and screenshot-ready.
  • Premium positioning. Up to $295 sustains higher AOV and a fashion-not-footwear perception.
  • Washability. "Throw them in the machine" is a single, sticky, demoable benefit nobody else owns.
  • Aesthetic loyalty. Polished workwear/wedding use-cases drive repeat purchase across colorways.
