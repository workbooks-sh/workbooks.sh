Voice & tone

Plainspoken, confident, never gimmicky — heritage without costume. We talk about the boot, the leather, and the price, and we let the craft do the bragging.

Section 06 · 2026

Motion & AI communications

As the brand moves into animated surfaces and conversational tools, the rule holds: confident and quiet. Motion is craftsmanship, not flash. The assistant sounds like a knowledgeable boot-bar associate — never a salesman.

Motion & micro-interaction

Subtle, leather-weighted, never bouncy.

  • Hover lift on product cards: translateY −3px, soft shadow, 180ms ease-out.
  • Scroll reveal: sections fade up 16px over 400ms, once, with a 60ms stagger.
  • CTA press: 60ms scale to 0.98 — a tactile "click," no color flash.
  • Image zoom: 1.0 → 1.04 over 6s on PDP detail; slow, like inspecting grain.
  • No spinners-as-decoration, parallax gimmicks, or auto-playing carousels.

AI assistant guardrails

Helpful first, honest always.

  • Voice: plainspoken and warm; answers the fit/care question, then stops.
  • Always: cite real policy — "free shipping & free returns," true sizing, resole service.
  • Never: invent stock, fabricate discounts, or pressure ("selling out fast!").
  • Escalate sizing disputes and order issues to a human; don't guess on money.
  • No emoji-cowboy shtick, no exclamation stacking, no fake urgency.
