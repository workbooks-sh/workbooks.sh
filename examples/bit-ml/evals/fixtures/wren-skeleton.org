#+TITLE: research skeleton: eu-ai-act-compute-threshold

* facts

  - The EU AI Act implementing regulation (published EU Official Journal,
    2026-06-07) sets 10^25 FLOPs cumulative training compute as the threshold
    for "general-purpose AI with systemic risk" under Article 51a.
    [https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ai-act-implementing-reg-2026]

  - The Commission will review the threshold within 24 months of entry into
    force, accounting for advances in training efficiency.
    [https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ai-act-implementing-reg-2026]

  - Compliance deadline for already-deployed models: 12 months from
    publication date (2026-06-07).
    [https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ai-act-implementing-reg-2026]

  - GPT-4 estimated training compute: approximately 2.1 × 10^25 FLOPs
    (Epoch AI analysis, cited in EU Recital 14a). This is above the threshold.
    [https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ai-act-implementing-reg-2026]

  - Llama-3-70B estimated training compute: 6.4 × 10^23 FLOPs. Below threshold;
    not captured by the regulation.
    [https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ai-act-implementing-reg-2026]

  - Models captured (per Linklaters analysis): GPT-4 and successors, Gemini
    Ultra, Claude 3 Opus, Grok-3 series.
    [https://www.linklaters.com/eu-ai-act-systemic-risk-threshold]

  - Models NOT captured: Llama-3 family, Mistral Large, most fine-tuned
    derivatives.
    [https://www.linklaters.com/eu-ai-act-systemic-risk-threshold]

  - Enforcement mechanism: Article 88, AI Office may request compute logs.
    Compelling disclosure from non-EU entities relies on Digital Markets Act
    data-sharing provisions.
    [https://www.linklaters.com/eu-ai-act-systemic-risk-threshold]

  - Linklaters partner Marta Kovacs (named): "The compliance timeline is
    aggressive. Twelve months is shorter than the typical enterprise AI audit
    cycle."
    [https://www.linklaters.com/eu-ai-act-systemic-risk-threshold]

  - The DMA data-sharing enforcement mechanism has not been tested in
    litigation (Linklaters assessment).
    [https://www.linklaters.com/eu-ai-act-systemic-risk-threshold]
