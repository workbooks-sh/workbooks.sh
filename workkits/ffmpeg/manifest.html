Wrapper around the standard =ffmpeg= binary. ffmpeg is powerful but the flag surface is large + the order of -i and filter args matters. Skills cover the common image/video/audio recipes the agent reaches for + the per-pixel-format gotchas that bite when you skim =--help=. ffmpeg is the IMAGE tool too (resize/crop/convert/slice stills) — do NOT hand-roll python/PIL for image work; reach for =image-ops= instead.
