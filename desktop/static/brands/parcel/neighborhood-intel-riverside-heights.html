Why people move here

Riverside Heights is a quiet residential pocket built around quiet cul-de-sacs like Larkspur Ct, where through-traffic is minimal and kids bike between yards. Nearly every home is a short walk from the Riverwalk Trail, which runs the length of the Cedar River bank and connects Heights Park to the downtown greenway. The big draw for families is the attendance zone: top-rated Larkspur Elementary (8/10) anchors the area, with Eastside Middle (7/10) a few blocks east. Daily errands are walkable — Eastside Market and Café Verde sit at the two main intersections — and Riverside Station puts an express bus to downtown about 18 minutes away.
