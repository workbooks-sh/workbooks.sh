Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Autopoet ("Autopoet", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use autopoet.app, the Autopoet application and assistant, and related services (the "Services"), including our SMS/text messaging program. Autopoet is operated by shinyobjectz, 968 NE Paula Drive, Bend, OR 97701, United States. Questions: shane@zaius.ai.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and authentication identifiers when you create an account or sign in.
  • Mobile phone number and messaging data — when you link your mobile number to chat with your assistant by text, we collect your phone number, your opt-in consent record, the messages you exchange with the assistant, and delivery metadata (timestamps, carrier status).
  • Content you create — the documents, notes, tasks, and configuration you author or that your assistant maintains on your behalf.
  • Connected-account data — when you choose to connect a third-party account, we access only the data covered by the permissions you grant, solely to provide the features you asked for.
  • Usage and device data — log data, IP address, browser and device type, and interaction events, used to operate and secure the Services.

SMS / text messaging program

Our messaging program lets you text your AI assistant from your own mobile number. Participation is opt-in: you provide your own mobile number, check an explicit consent box, and confirm ownership via a one-time SMS passcode (double opt-in) before any conversational messages are sent. Message frequency is conversational and varies with your use. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time; STOP is honored automatically and permanently. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact shane@zaius.ai.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in consent, or the contents of your messages with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing. We use it only to operate the messaging program you enrolled in — routing your messages to and from your assistant through our messaging carrier — and to comply with law.

How we use information

We use information to operate, maintain, and improve the Services; to authenticate you; to route and deliver the messages you exchange with your assistant; to perform the actions you request; to provide support; to detect and prevent fraud and abuse; and to comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

  • Service providers — vendors who host, process, or support the Services under contract, limited to what they need to perform their work. This includes our messaging carrier (Telnyx), used solely to deliver SMS to and from your number. These providers are contractually restricted from using your data for their own purposes.
  • Platforms you connect — to perform an action you request, we transmit the necessary data to a platform you have connected.
  • Legal and safety — where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or Autopoet.

We do not share connected-platform data or messaging data with third parties for their own marketing.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, then delete or de-identify it, except where a longer period is required by law. You may request deletion at any time — see our Data Deletion page.

Your choices and rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your information, unlink your mobile number, and disconnect any connected account at any time from your account settings or by replying STOP to messages. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; contact us to exercise them.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information, including encryption of credentials and connection tokens at rest. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International users

Autopoet is operated from the United States. If you use the Services from outside the United States, you consent to processing your information in the United States and other countries where we or our providers operate.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date, and where appropriate we will notify you.
