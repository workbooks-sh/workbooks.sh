Data Deletion

Effective date: July 5, 2026

You can ask us to delete the data associated with your Autopoet account, your linked mobile number and messaging history, and any accounts you have connected, at any time. This page explains how.

How to request deletion

Email shane@zaius.ai from the address on your account with the subject line "Delete my data", or use the in-app control at Settings → Account → Delete my data. Please include your account email so we can locate your records. If you are requesting deletion of data tied to a specific connected platform or your linked phone number, tell us which one.

What we delete

Upon a verified request we delete: your account and profile information; the documents and content you created; your linked mobile number, SMS opt-in consent record, and message history; and the access tokens and any cached data obtained from connected platforms. Some information may be retained where a longer period is required by law (for example, financial records) or in secure backups that are overwritten on a rolling basis.

Timeframe

We confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete deletion within 30 days. We will email you when the deletion is complete.

Stopping SMS without deleting your account

To stop text messages without deleting your whole account, reply STOP to any message, or unlink your number at Settings → Channels. STOP is honored automatically and permanently.

Disconnecting a single platform

To revoke our access to a connected platform without deleting your whole account, open Settings → Connections and disconnect that platform. This immediately revokes our access to that platform's data going forward.
