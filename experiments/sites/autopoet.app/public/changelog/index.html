The nervous system validated

The learning layer, proven end to end on deterministic seeds and production paths — no gradients, no GPU.

  • Hebbian plasticity on the workspace graph: next-access prediction 0.783 vs 0.757 baseline; recovery after drift in 500 events vs 622.
  • Surprise-gated cognition: quality 1.000 at 1.3% of always-model cost; attention spikes 4.7× at regime switches and self-quenches.
  • The economy: 0.970 on the canonical benchmark from outcome-only credit; two layers invent a communication protocol under a 2-bit channel (0.947).
  • The gym: 8,076 events through real parse, hook, and event-dispatch paths — 100% effect settlement, p50 3µs.
